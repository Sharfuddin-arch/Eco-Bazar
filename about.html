About Eco Bazaar

Rooted in nature • backed by trust & certifications

Founded in 2024, Eco Bazaar emerged from a powerful mission: authentic organic products, absolute transparency, and farmer-first ethics. In a market with misleading labels, we pioneered the Transparency Card™ – trace your product from seed to shelf.

Today, we partner with 50+ certified organic farms across Pakistan, from the fertile Punjab fields to pristine Gilgit valleys. We've eliminated 15,000+ kg of single-use plastic through zero-waste packaging. But our proudest achievement is your trust — backed by verifiable international certifications and direct farm linkages.

Eco Bazaar is ISO 22000:2018 & HACCP certified for food safety, and we're proud members of the Organic & Natural Products Association (ONPA). Every item you buy meets Pakistan National Organic Standards & global equivalence.
